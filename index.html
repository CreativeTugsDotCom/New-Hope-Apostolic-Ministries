OUR MINISTRY

More Than a Place to Worship

New Hope Apostolic Ministries is a community where faith, family, and relationships come together. Led by a community committed to faith, family, and connection, our goal is to create an environment where people of different ages can connect, feel welcomed, and experience a sense of belonging.

For families, that means creating opportunities for children and young people to meet others, participate in activities, enjoy time together, and discover a community where faith can become part of everyday life.

Your First Visit Should Feel Easy.
